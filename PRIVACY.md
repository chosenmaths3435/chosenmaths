# Privacy policy

The scripts in this repository are used by the channel owner to publish their own videos
to their own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and Bluesky.

- They act only on accounts the owner controls, with permissions the owner granted.
- They collect, store, and share no data about anyone else.
- Access tokens are kept on the owner's computer and are never sent anywhere except the
  platform they belong to.

To remove the app's access, revoke it in the platform's account settings (for example,
Google Account -> Security -> Third-party access, or Facebook -> Settings -> Apps and
websites). Questions: open an issue on this repository.
